## Scope and how we apply it

- Permission is for what was requested: Argentine first division results and final tables, in a free, non-commercial app. Commercial use and anything else (other competitions, other countries) need a new written answer.
- The reply comes from an RSSSF contact. Individual pages still carry their own author and rights notice, so the credit names the author shown on each page.
- Every record taken from RSSSF keeps the URL of the page used in its `source` field.
- `/acerca` shows the credit line with the author of the page used and a link to RSSSF.
