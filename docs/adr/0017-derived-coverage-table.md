# 0017 — The coverage table is derived from the dataset

Status: **Accepted** (2026-10-17). Written with plan 0014 (issue #12). Decisions 1 to 3 and 6 to 8 are technical and follow from ADRs 0003, 0011 and 0015. Diego approved decision 4 (stretches follow participation, D1), decision 5 (gaps are explained in the reviewed gap list and the methodology, with no schema change, D3) and the registration of the competitions that are not loaded (D2, Phase 2 of the plan) on 2026-10-17.

## Context

`data/coverage.json` tells the statistics engine where the data is complete (ADR 0003, methodology "Partial coverage"). The engine (plan 0005) merges the `complete` rows of a club and competition into stretches and marks each listed competition `complete`, `partial` or `missing`; `complete: false` rows only make a competition listed. The file does not exist yet, so every real period is reported as `missing` or `partial`, which is safe but useless.

The real dataset has 34 clubs, 2 competitions, 8 seasons and 2,435 matches: the main league tournament of each year from 2018-19 to 2024, the Copa Diego Maradona 2020 and the 2025 Apertura until 2025-05-04 (its season ends 2025-06-01). Clubs enter and leave the top flight (Arsenal plays 2019-20 to 2023, San Martín de Tucumán only 2018-19, Belgrano 2018-19 and from 2023). Other competitions of the same years (Copa de la Liga 2021 to 2024, Copa Argentina, Libertadores, Sudamericana, super cups) are not loaded.

Writing about 230 rows by hand, and keeping them right after every ingestion, invites exactly the error the project forbids: presenting incomplete data as complete. Most of what a row says can be computed from `seasons.json` and `matches.json`; the rest (which competitions or editions exist and are not loaded) is a reviewed fact that needs a source.

## Decision

1. **Meaning of a row.** `complete: true` over `[from, to]` asserts that every match the club played in that competition dated inside the interval is in `matches.json`. `complete: false` asserts that the club may have played that competition in the interval and the data is missing or incomplete; it is never evidence of completeness. A day with no row is unknown and is treated as not complete. `to: null` means "until today"; the generator only emits it on `complete: false` rows while no live source exists (ADR 0003, open decision).
2. **Derived, not hand-written.** `data/coverage.json` is the output of a pure function `generateCoverage(dataset, gaps)` in `src/data-model/`. Its inputs are `clubs`, `competitions`, `seasons` and `matches`, plus a reviewed list of **declared gaps** (`src/data-model/coverage-gaps.ts`): per competition, an interval that is known not to be loaded, with a `source` and an English `reason`. `pnpm data:coverage` writes the file. Hand edits are not allowed; a test asserts that the committed file equals the generator output.
3. **Season load.** A season with at least one match is loaded. It is complete when its last match date equals its `end` (the convention of ADR 0015, decision 8: a complete file's season ends on its last match; a partial file's season end comes from a cited source), otherwise partial and loaded through its last match date. A partial season's missing tail must fall inside a declared gap, or the generator fails. A season with no match is not loaded.
4. **Complete stretches follow participation (needs Diego, D1).** For each club and competition, the seasons of the competition are sorted by start. A complete stretch is a maximal run of consecutive seasons that are loaded and in which the club played at least one match; it runs from the first season's `start` to the last season's loaded-through date. A season the club did not play, or a season not loaded, ends the stretch. Declared gaps are subtracted from the stretches. This generalizes ADR 0003, decision 1 ("coverage starts at the later of 2018-19 and the club's first season in the dataset") to every promotion and relegation, and never claims completeness across a period in which the club was outside the competition.
5. **Gaps are explained in the reviewed gap list, not in the rows (needs Diego, D3).** Each declared gap produces one `complete: false` row for **every** club, with the gap's `source`. The `reason` lives in the gap list and in the methodology's table of known gaps. The coverage schema and the Supabase table do not change.
6. **Every pair has a row.** Every club and competition pair gets at least one row. A pair with no stretch and no gap gets a fallback row `complete: false` from the competition's first season start to `null`. A competition with no loaded season must have a declared gap, or the generator fails. Complete rows have `source: "derived:<first season id>..<last season id>"` (each season keeps its own source).
7. **Validator rules (data only, no gap list needed).** `missing-coverage`: a club and competition pair has no row. `coverage-overlap`: two rows of the same pair share a day. `coverage-overclaim`: a complete row shares a day with a season of its competition that has no match. The exact agreement between the file and the data is enforced by the drift test, not by `validate()`, because the validator does not see the gap list.
8. **Ingestion regenerates coverage.** Every adapter run (ADR 0015) regenerates `coverage.json` from the merged dataset before validation and writes it in the same commit. This amends ADR 0015, decision 3: `coverage.json` is owned by no adapter and is rebuilt as a whole on every run.

## Consequences

- The engine and its rules do not change: it already reads `complete` rows as stretches. A fan start date before a club's first stretch shows up as `partial` with `completeFrom` after the period start; that is what the UI notice uses (Fase 3).
- The partial 2025 Apertura and the Copa de la Liga editions that are not loaded become visible in the data, as plans 0011 and 0013 asked.
- `coverage.json` is about 230 rows today and grows with clubs times gaps. Adding a club or a competition by hand requires `pnpm data:coverage`; `missing-coverage` reminds the author.
- The Copa Diego Maradona 2020 is not "fully covered" for the validator's season rules, because its last match (2021-03-31) falls inside the Copa de la Liga 2021 gap. `title-without-support` and `standings-mismatch` skip that season. This is the safe direction.
- A club absent from some seasons gets a `partial` or `missing` warning for a period in which it played elsewhere (for example the Primera Nacional). That is a deliberate, safe false warning; recorded promotions and relegations (`movements.json`) can later let the generator bridge such stretches, through a new ADR.
- Competitions that are not in `competitions.json` are invisible to the engine, so `isComplete` stays "complete for the listed competitions" (methodology, UI notes) until those competitions are recorded with gaps (plan 0014, D2).
- Season-precision matches (ADR 0011, issue #38) have no date: when they land, decision 3 must use the season `end` as loaded-through, with its own tests.
- If Diego prefers continuous stretches (plan 0014, D1, rejected alternative) or a `notes` column (D3, rejected alternative), this ADR is amended before the build; D3's alternative also needs a Supabase migration and the schema parity test.
