# 0020 — RSSSF ingestion: pinned excerpts, layout profiles and season ownership

Status: **Accepted** (2026-10-23). Written with plan 0017 (issue #70). Diego approved D1 to D9 of the plan as recommended and changed D10 (the vitrina is fed by RSSSF and by every permitted source). Decisions 1 to 4, 6 and 9 follow from ADRs 0003, 0011, 0012, 0015 and 0017; decisions 5, 7 and 8 were confirmed by those answers. Built and checked on the real 2025 page (see the verification log of the plan).

## Context

RSSSF approved in writing (2026-10-16, `docs/spike/replies/2026-10-16-rsssf.md`, ADR 0003 decision 6) storing the **results and final tables of the Argentine first division** in this public repository with a per-record source, showing derived statistics in the free app, and crediting RSSSF and the page author. Other competitions need a new written answer.

ADR 0015 was written for openfootball, a GitHub repository with commits, plain-text files in one grammar and a CC0 license. RSSSF pages are different in every way that matters for ingestion. What we measured on 2026-10-19 (10 requests, plan 0017 "Research log"):

- **No revisions.** A page is edited in place ("Last updated: 7 Oct 2026" on `arg2025.html`); there is no permalink to an older version. A commit SHA cannot be pinned.
- **One page, many competitions.** A season page holds the first division and also the Copa Argentina, the Copa de la Superliga, super cups, the lower divisions and the national teams (`arg2019.html` is 622 KB). Only the first-division sections are inside the permission.
- **Several layouts.** Each era is written by a different author in a different layout: score `3:3` with one date per round (1950), tab-separated lines with scorers under each match (1971), plain lines with `[Aug 21]` date headers and `abd` for abandoned matches (2009-10), and full legal names with stadiums, `[4] 0- 0 [2]` shoot-outs, `aet` and `abandoned ... remaining time on 24 Oct` (2017 onward).
- **Legacy encodings, unreliable headers.** Pages are windows-1252 or ISO-8859-1. One header declares `charset=UTFs-8`; another declares none. The current loader decodes strict UTF-8 and would reject every page.
- **Dates without years.** Match dates are `[Mon DD]` or `[Mon DD, Wkd]`, and postponed or advanced matches appear out of order.
- **Names differ inside one page.** Final tables use one spelling (`Estudiantes (La Plata)`, `Club Atlético San Martín` plus a headquarters column) and match lines another (`Estudiantes LP`, `CA San Martín (San Juan)`), and match lines themselves vary (`Racing` and `Racing Club` in the same tournament). The same-season spelling check of ADR 0012 (decision 3) would fail on every club.
- **Overlap.** RSSSF also covers 2018-19 to 2025, which openfootball already owns, and goes past openfootball's last match (2025-05-04) through the 2025 Clausura and the 2026 Apertura.

## Decision

1. **Pinned excerpts are the input.** The adapter reads **byte-exact excerpts** of the first-division sections of RSSSF pages, committed under `sources/rsssf/` with a reviewed `manifest.json`. For each page the manifest records the URL, the retrieval date, the sha256 and byte length of the **whole page** as fetched, the encoding, the author names and the "Last updated" line shown on the page, the layout id (decision 3), and for each excerpt its start and end markers and its own sha256. The page sha256 plus the retrieval date is the pin (there is no revision to pin); the committed excerpt is what makes a run reproducible after the page changes. Excerpts are cut from the page bytes, never re-encoded or edited; the "About this document" block (which holds the authors' e-mail addresses) is never inside an excerpt.
2. **Only one tool talks to rsssf.org.** A snapshot script (`pnpm data:snapshot rsssf`, I/O only, run by a person, never in CI) fetches the listed pages one at a time with at least 5 seconds between requests and a neutral user agent without personal data, and writes the excerpts and the manifest. `--check` only reports which pages changed. The ingestion adapter reads `sources/rsssf/` by default; with `--network` it fetches the live page and must reproduce the pinned page sha256 before cutting the excerpt, so a changed page stops the run. The fetch host allowlist of `pinned-fetch` becomes a per-adapter list (`www.rsssf.org` for this adapter), still https-only, with redirects refused, a timeout and a size cap.
3. **Layout profiles, strict grammar, loud failure.** Each page is assigned one layout id in the manifest. A layout is a parser with an exhaustive line grammar: every line of an excerpt is a heading, a date header, a match, a table row, a note it recognizes, or a line listed in the page's reviewed `ignore` list (with the reason). Anything else stops the run with the excerpt name and line. A page whose layout is unknown is not ingested; adding a layout is a code change with its own tests.
4. **Dates are resolved inside a sourced window.** Each tournament in the adapter config has a date window (first and last calendar year, and the month that splits them). A `[Mon DD]` date maps to the only year of the window in which it falls; when the line has a weekday, the weekday must match. A date outside the window, or ambiguous, stops the run. Nothing is rounded, guessed or carried over from the previous line.
5. **Ownership and one source per season.** The source prefix is `https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/`. The adapter owns the matches, standings and titles whose `source` starts with it; the ownership merge of ADR 0015 (decision 3) is extended from matches to `standings.json` and `titles.json`. **A season's matches come from one source**: the adapter never emits a match in a season that already has matches from another source, except a reviewed `extends` entry that adds only matches dated strictly after that source's last match of the season and reuses its season record deep-equal (plan 0017, D3: the 2025 Apertura knockout stage). Any other overlap is a conflict that stops the run. Seasons owned by another source are parsed only for a read-only cross-check report; nothing from that comparison is written. Every record's `source` is the page URL plus the anchor of the tournament section (`https://www.rsssf.org/tablesa/arg2010.html#1apertura`); the excerpt name and line are in the run report.
6. **Internal consistency replaces header counts.** RSSSF pages have no match-count header. For every tournament whose final table covers all its matches, the adapter tallies played, won, drawn, lost, goals for and goals against per club from the parsed matches and compares them with the table row, plus the total number of matches. A difference stops the run unless it is listed in the page's reviewed `tableExceptions` with a reason (for example a match awarded by the league after the table was built). This is the check the methodology asks of every new source ("a season whose first matches are missing still reads as complete").
7. **Club names: exact aliases, scoped spelling check.** Names resolve only through `resolveClubId` (ADR 0012, decision 2). Table rows resolve the exact string `<name> (<headquarters>)` when the layout has a headquarters column, because the bare names repeat (three clubs called Huracán in the 1971 Nacional, two Club Atlético San Martín in 2018-19). ADR 0012 decision 3 is amended for this adapter: the "two spellings of one club in one season" check runs per season **and per context** (match lines, table rows), and a spelling pair that really occurs in one context passes only when the page's reviewed `variants` list names it (`Racing` and `Racing Club` in the 2009 Apertura). Unknown names are collected and reported together, as ADR 0015 decision 6 says.
8. **Records.** Matches have day precision (ADR 0011). An abandoned match that was completed later is **one** record with the final score, dated on the day it was completed; an abandoned match never completed is `suspended` with no score; a match awarded by the league is `administrative_result` dated on its scheduled day; an awarded match without a date on the page stops the run (season precision, issue #38, is not implemented). Home and away are as listed, also at neutral venues. Standings rows are emitted only for tournaments whose table covers the whole season. Titles, if Diego approves them for this adapter (plan 0017, D5), come from the champion marked on the season page, must equal the champion in RSSSF's list of champions, and use the id `<season_id>-<club_id>`; this amends ADR 0003 decision 4 for the first division only.
9. **Credit is generated.** `/acerca` builds the RSSSF credit from the manifest: every author of a page used, once, with a link to the RSSSF main page and to the pages used, as the reply asks. A page cannot be added to the manifest without its author.

## Consequences

- The repository holds copies of the first-division sections of the RSSSF pages used, never whole pages; cup and lower-division text is never committed before a written reply covers it (issue #70, Phase B). **Update 2026-10-23:** RSSSF answered that any data needed may be used with a citation of the dataset owner (`docs/spike/replies/2026-10-23-rsssf-all-data.md`), so cups are inside the permission; the per-page author credit and the per-record `source` still apply.
- RSSSF pages change, so the network path will often stop with a hash mismatch; that is the intended signal. Picking up corrections is a normal data PR: run the snapshot script, review the excerpt diff, re-run the adapter, review the `data/` diff.
- Each new era costs a layout parser, its fixtures and the clubs of that era (ADR 0016). Eras are added one at a time, in their own PRs.
- The Kaggle adapter (ADR 0003 decision 2, issue #38) must not load seasons that RSSSF owns: day precision wins (ADR 0011 decision 6), and decision 5 stops a second source in the same season anyway.
- `standings.json` and `titles.json` appear in `data/` for the first time; the validator's `standings-mismatch` and `title-without-support` rules start to run on real data.
- Club aliases grow to several spellings per club (match lines and table rows of each layout). Alias uniqueness after folding (ADR 0012 decision 3) still applies to all of them.
- If Diego rejects the excerpts (plan 0017, D4), runs are reproducible only from a private archive of the pages, and this ADR is amended before the build.
